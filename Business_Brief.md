# One-Page Business Brief: 2025 Sales Review

**Prepared by:** Your Name | **Data:** Generated (simulated) e-commerce orders, 1 Jan to 31 Dec 2025

## Context
Management wants to know which months and categories earn the most, who the best customers are, and what to do next. This review covers 2,000 orders from 300 customers across four categories (Electronics, Fashion, Home, Books), with total revenue of about **USD 2.40 million**.

> The data is simulated for practice. Patterns should be confirmed with real data before big decisions.

## Key findings
1. **Revenue slipped in the second half.** H2 earned about 6.9% less than H1 (USD 1.16 million vs USD 1.24 million). Quarterly revenue fell from USD 632k (Q1) to USD 577k (Q4).
2. **February was the best month (USD 232k) and August the worst (USD 180k).** August fell 18.9% from July, mostly because Home dropped from about USD 41k to USD 19k.
3. **Fashion and Electronics bring 65.6% of revenue** (Fashion 35.5%, Electronics 30.1%). Average order value is almost the same in every category (about USD 1,160 to USD 1,220), so the gap comes from the number of orders (Fashion 700, Books 298).
4. **Revenue is spread widely across customers.** The top 10% (30 customers) bring only 18.9% of revenue, and it takes 190 of 300 customers to reach 80%.
5. **The base is loyal but 52 customers (17.3%) are inactive.** Everyone ordered at least twice and 78% ordered five or more times. 52 customers have not ordered in 90 days; their past spend totals about USD 314k (13.0% of revenue).

## Recommendations
1. **Run an offer in July and August to lift the second half.** Closing even half of the H2 shortfall would add about USD 43k.
2. **Cross-sell Books and Home to Fashion and Electronics buyers.** Each extra order is worth about USD 1,200 in any category, so more orders in the smaller categories adds revenue directly.
3. **Use a loyalty programme for all customers, plus a win-back email to the 52 inactive ones.** A VIP-only scheme for the top 10% would reach just 18.9% of revenue.

## Next steps
- Repeat the analysis on real order data to confirm the August dip and the category pattern.
- Test the July/August offer on a small group and compare its orders with a control group.
- Track the 90-day inactive list monthly and measure how many return after the win-back email.
